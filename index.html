Edit thresholds
Configure grade tiers, points and per-period overrides for CSAT, AR, AHT and your custom metrics.
›
Built-in metrics (CSAT, AR, AHT)
Choose where each built-in metric counts toward the Final KPI: the main dashboard (the whole team), the team members' tiles and dashboards, or both. A metric that's left out still shows its tile and grade; it just adds 0 points to the Final KPI on that side.
Custom KPI metrics
Add your own metrics alongside CSAT, AR and AHT — a name, a unit, which direction is better, and an optional target. Choose where each metric counts: the main dashboard (the whole team), the team members' tiles and dashboards, or both. Each new metric gets its own tab in Edit thresholds, where you set its grade cutoffs and (in KPI Weights) the points per grade. Nothing is adjusted for you. Remove one anytime; values you've already logged stay in your data.
